THE GAME

Two players, one webcam

Side by side, 2.5 m back. The person on the left is P1, on the right P2: the same sides as their screen halves.

Your legs are the engine

Run in place to gallop. Stop, and your unicorn slows to a trot.

Lean, jump, hands up

Lean your upper body to steer, jump on the striped line, raise both hands to fire a rainbow dash.

Three laps of rainbow road

Rainbows fill the dash meter; rocks and logs make you tumble.

One screen, split in two halves, one webcam. Each player stands on their side of the room, which is also their side of the screen. There is no controller: running in place is the accelerator, leaning the upper body is the steering wheel, a real jump hops obstacles, and both hands up is the item button. Keyboards work too, and an empty seat is taken by a CPU unicorn.
