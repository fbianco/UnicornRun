KEEPING IT STEADY

Smoothing

Each point is averaged, 60 % new and 40 % old: less jitter for about one frame of delay.

Dead zones

Small sways do not steer, and walking or drifting across the room never does.

Visibility

Unsure shoulders mean “not seen”. Hips are only used when the camera sees them.

Arms are not jumps

With the hips out of view, raising your arms lifts the shoulders but never counts as a jump.

Auto-pause

A player out of view for 1.5 s pauses the race; 0.6 s back in view resumes it.

Tested without a camera

45 tests: synthetic skeletons, the jump line at every speed, the training, whole races.

Body control only feels good if it is predictable. These are the small rules that make it so. The most useful one in practice is the test suite: synthetic skeletons are built in code, frame by frame, so we can check “running in place for five seconds never jumps”, “drifting a whole shoulder width while running never steers” or “P1 keeps their slot when P2 leaves” in milliseconds, without anybody standing in front of a camera.
