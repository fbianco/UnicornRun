33 POINTS, 9 THAT MATTER

● Shoulders · their midpoint over the hips is your lean; their distance is the ruler for everything else.

● Hips · body height for the jump and the running bounce; the base of your lean.

● Wrists · hands up for join and dash; an X to skip; arm pumping.

● Knees · they alternate up and down when you run in place.

● Nose · the line both hands must pass for “hands up”.

Each point has x and y (0 to 1 across the picture) and a visibility score. Points the model is unsure about (under 0.5) are ignored.

MediaPipe gives 33 landmarks per person: face points, shoulders, elbows, wrists, fingers, hips, knees, ankles and feet. The game only needs nine of them. Shoulders are the most important: where their middle sits over the middle of the hips tells us how far you lean, and the distance between them is our ruler. Hips give body height for jumps. Wrists and nose give hands up, and crossed wrists skip the training. Knees and wrists help detect running in place.
