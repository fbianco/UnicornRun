Skip with an X

Cross your arms in front of you for 1 s. Rematches skip it; new players get it.

New body players now go through a short training before the race, each in their own half of the screen. Five steps: lean left, lean right, run in place, jump twice, and fire a rainbow dash, for which the meter is filled for you. It uses the real race physics on an empty straight road, so nothing can go wrong, and the steering slider shows a green target zone to reach. Progress never resets, so a child who wobbles still gets there. Children who already know the game cross their arms in front of them for a second to skip it. Whoever finishes first can keep practising until the other player is done; then the race starts on the start line.
