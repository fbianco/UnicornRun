TWO CAMERAS, ONE CANVAS

Two RoadViews, each clipped to its 640 × 720 half, each with its own camera.

The rival is just one more sprite on its slice, with a name tag.

HUD on the outer edges; the minimap in the middle is the curves added up into an outline.

Particles live in each half’s screen space.

Split screen is surprisingly cheap with this design. A RoadView is “the world as seen from behind one runner”. We create two of them, clip each to its half of the canvas and draw them one after the other. Each has its own background scroll and its own sparkles, so a rainbow P1 collects sparkles only on P1’s side. The other player is not special: they are placed on their slice like any tree. The minimap is computed once by adding up the curve values into a top-down outline and closing the small error.
