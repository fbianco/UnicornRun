LEAN, DON’T WALK

Before: shoulders vs. a spot in the room. Kids drift while running, so they steered without leaning.

Now: shoulders over hips. Only a real lean steers, anywhere on your half.

Measured on the body

Lean = hip centre minus shoulder centre, in shoulder widths. Hips out of view: the slope of the shoulder line instead.

Straight is a zone

Under 0.15 nothing happens; 0.55, about 20°, is a full turn. A lean while calibrating is trusted only up to 0.2.

See your lean

A slider at the bottom of each half: the dot is you, the grey middle is “straight”, the arrows light up when the unicorn turns.

The first version measured your shoulders against the spot where you calibrated. In a playtest, children who ran in place slowly wandered sideways, and their unicorn kept turning although they stood straight. Now steering is measured on the body itself: how far the middle of the shoulders sits sideways over the middle of the hips, in shoulder widths, like a surfer. Where you stand no longer matters. If the camera cannot see the hips, the slope of the shoulder line gives the same number. The slider at the bottom of the screen shows the lean live, also inside the dead zone, so children see what the game sees and learn quickly how much to lean.
