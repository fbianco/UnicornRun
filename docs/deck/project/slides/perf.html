The numbers

5.5MS

to draw both views; the budget at 60 fps is 16.7 ms

230

things per view per frame: about 100 road slices and 130 sprites

45

automated tests, including whole simulated races

Drawing time measured in headless Chrome without a graphics card, averaged over 120 frames.

Plain 2D canvas is plenty. Both views together take about five and a half milliseconds, a third of the frame budget, and that was measured without a graphics card, so a normal laptop has lots of headroom left for MediaPipe, which also wants the GPU. The 45 tests cover the gesture rules, player assignment, the track, the jump line timing, the training steps and full races between CPU unicorns.
