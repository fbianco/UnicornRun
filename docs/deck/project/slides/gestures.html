Here are all the gestures the game understands. Steering is analogue: a small lean steers a little, a big lean steers fully, and there is a dead zone so you can go straight while swaying. The lean is the shoulders over the hips, so walking around does not steer. Running in place is measured three ways because cameras see bodies differently: hip bounce always works, knees only if the camera sees them, arm pumping as a bonus. The strongest wins. A jump must be both high and fast, which is what separates it from standing on your toes. Hands up joins the race in the lobby, and fires the rainbow dash once per raise during the race. Crossing your arms in front of you skips the training.
