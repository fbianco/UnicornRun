MEASURED IN SHOULDER WIDTHS

lean = (hip centre x − shoulder centre x)
      ÷ shoulder width

Calibrate

After joining, stand up straight with arms down for 1.5 s. The median of those frames becomes your ruler, your height and your “straight”.

Same rules at 2 m or 3 m

Every threshold is a fraction of your own shoulder width, so distance and body size cancel out.

Follow slowly

While you stand still, your normal height re-centres 2 % per frame; the ruler adapts 1 % if you step closer.

The lobby: raise both hands to join, then stand still. Ready players can test the steering slider and the run meter live.

The single most important idea, inherited from Bun Rush: never use raw pixels. A child standing three metres away and an adult at two metres produce very different numbers, but both have shoulders. So every distance is divided by the player’s own shoulder width. Calibration records your neutral pose: it waits until your hands are down, takes about 45 frames and keeps the median, which ignores glitchy frames. The lean itself is shoulders over hips, divided by the same ruler, so it does not matter where in the room you stand. Your normal height follows you very slowly, but only while you are standing still, so a jump is never “absorbed”.
