BAKED ONCE, DRAWN OFTEN

Baked

21 scenery painters drawn once at 2× size, then copied with drawImage at any scale.

Live

Unicorns are drawn every frame: gallop phase, lean, jump, tumble, dash glow.

Backgrounds

Sky and 3 tileable layers, pre-rendered at the real pixel size.

There are no image files in the game: every picture is drawn with paths and gradients. Drawing a candy tree from vectors 130 times per frame would be slow, so each scenery painter is run once at start-up into an offscreen canvas, at twice its size so close-ups stay sharp. After that, a tree costs one drawImage call at whatever size the projection asks for. The unicorns are the exception: there are only two or three on screen and they animate, so they are drawn live every frame from the gallop phase, the steering lean, the jump height and the tumble.
